What's Included

Explore the Local Area

Our dive trips launch from the scenic South Devon coast. You'll get time to enjoy local beaches, small fishing villages, and cliffside views.

See Local Sea Life

Expect to encounter colourful fish, starfish, crabs, and maybe even a curious seal. Great for underwater photography and wildlife lovers.

Accessibility

We welcome all guests. Our boats are equipped with easy-access steps and we offer assistance for anyone with mobility needs. Let us know in advance.

Food & Refreshments

Each trip includes a packed lunch with local produce, water, and tea or coffee on deck. Vegetarian and allergy-friendly options available.
