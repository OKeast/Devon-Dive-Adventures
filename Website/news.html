Lundy is an island in the Bristol Channel between England and Wales on the west coast of Britain.


The Field Society has been offering Conservation Breaks to Lundy since 1984


It attracts many visitors, both for its peaceful surroundings and for scientific research while offering free accomodation to those who aid in conservation efforts like path maintainence, drystone walling and cultivation work.
